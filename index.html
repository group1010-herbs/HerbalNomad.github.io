Our Story

Two Generations,
One Craft.

What started with a father cutting keys by the roadside in Kawangware became a legacy. Today, his son carries on — same spot, same precision, same promise. The machines are better. The craft is the same.

You won't come back because the key didn't work. That's not how Key & Son operates. Every key is tested before it leaves our hands — and between father and son, we've seen every lock, every blank, every tricky car chip.
