Foliar Biogenics

We brew our own
plant intelligence

Every foliar application at Kavutiri is crafted on-farm from medicinal and functional plants grown in our living buffer zones. No synthetic chemistry — only phytochemical precision validated by observable plant response.

🌳
Warburgia
Warburgia ugandensis

East African pepper-bark tree. Rich in sesquiterpene dialdehyde compounds with powerful antifungal and antibacterial action. Our extracts suppress coffee berry disease (CBD) fungal pressure when applied at canopy closure. Also serves as an insect deterrent via volatile compound emission.

🌵
Aloe Vera
Aloe barbadensis miller

Our aloe extract serves as a natural biostimulant carrier and surfactant. The mucilage coats leaf surfaces, prolonging active-ingredient contact time. Salicylic acid precursors in aloe activate plant systemic resistance (SAR), priming the coffee tree to defend itself before pathogen arrival.

🌸
Cape Periwinkle
Catharanthus roseus

Source of alkaloids including vindoline and catharanthine. Used in our foliar blend as a natural nematocide and growth modulator. Inter-planted throughout our orchard as a living mulch that also suppresses soil-borne nematode populations between fertigated rows.

🔬
Blend Protocol
Kavutiri Brew Method

Plant material is cold-macerated for 48 hours, then warm-fermented for 72 hours with activated EM (effective microorganisms). The resulting extract is diluted to field concentration, pH-adjusted to 6.2–6.5, and applied early morning during berry development and flowering stages.

Water & Aquaponics

Fish feed
our coffee trees

Freshwater fish ponds are integrated into our irrigation system, transforming water management from a delivery function into a nutrient cycling engine.

💧 Integrated Irrigation

Following the breakdown patterns identified in Kenya's national irrigation scholarship, Kavutiri operates a gravity-fed micro-scheme with timed delivery matching critical phenological windows: flowering (water stress avoided), berry swelling (peak demand), and post-harvest (minimum input). Scheduling is cross-referenced against live NDVI stress signals from our satellite feed.

🌊 Water Stewardship

We capture hillside runoff in lined retention ponds, reducing erosion while building a reserve buffer for dry-spell supplementary irrigation. All drainage is filtered through vetiver grass terraces before re-entering watercourses — a closed-loop that protects downstream ecologies.

🐟 Fish for Coffee

Our tilapia and catfish ponds are positioned uphill of the coffee rows. Nutrient-rich pond water — loaded with fish excreta (ammonia converted to bioavailable nitrate by nitrifying bacteria) — is timed into drip fertigation lines at weekly intervals during the growing season.

This delivers a slow-release nitrogen source without the salt-loading risk of synthetic fertilisers, while the fish consume plant debris introduced to the pond from pruning operations — closing the loop.

Reconstruction Programme

Five pillars
at farm scale

Drawing directly from the Ruiru Knowledge Network's 1956–57 operational model, Kavutiri implements all five pillars identified by current research as essential for sustained coffee productivity.

01 ·
Advisory Presence

Continuous on-farm advisory visits — mirroring the district-resident officer model — with structured observation protocols and direct farmer dialogue.

02 ·
Demonstration Farming

Treated vs. untreated comparison plots visible to neighbouring farmers, with transparent cost and yield data shared at open days.

03 ·
Knowledge Cadence

Monthly advisory bulletins and seasonal workshops using spaced-repetition principles to embed agronomic practice — not just awareness.

04 ·
Feedback Loops

Quarterly review committees with cooperative managers, pest scouts, and buyers — field observations informing research priorities.

05 ·
Premium Markets

Direct relationships with specialty roasters in Germany, the Nordic countries, and the United States — commercial proof embedded in the farm system.
